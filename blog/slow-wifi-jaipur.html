Slow Wi-Fi at home in Jaipur

2 Oct 2026 · SKYPC technicians

A slow connection is not always a reason to buy a new router. In a lot of Jaipur homes the line is fine in the room with the router and weak everywhere else. Run these checks before you book. If they do not change anything, a visit is the faster next step.

Check the router before you replace it

Restart the router and the modem, if they are two boxes, and leave them off for a full minute. Then test on one phone, standing next to the router, with mobile data switched off.

  • If it is fast beside the router and slow in other rooms, the line is probably fine and the signal is the problem.
  • If it is slow even beside the router, the connection or the router itself is the limit. A new placement will not fix that.
  • If it is fast in the morning and useless at night, too many devices, or the building's evening load, is more likely than a broken cable.

Note what you saw. That one sentence saves the technician from repeating the same test.

Placement and walls

Routers get hidden in a shoe rack, on the floor, or inside a metal cabinet because the box is ugly. Walls and metal block the signal. A router at about table height, nearer the middle of the home, usually beats a stronger router left in a corner cupboard.

Two-floor houses and long flats often need a second point, not a louder single router. That is a network visit: where the cable can run, and where the second unit should sit. It is not something a restart will solve.

Too many devices on one connection

Phones, a smart TV, a laptop and a CCTV recorder can all be online at once. The recorder in particular should be on a stable connection if you watch cameras away from home. See the CCTV guide if the cameras are the reason the Wi-Fi was set up.

Old passwords that neighbours or old staff still know will also fill the network. If you do not remember the last time the Wi-Fi password changed, change it, then reconnect only the devices you still use.

When a technician should visit

Book a visit when the speed is poor next to the router, when one floor has almost no signal, or when the office network drops every time a few people join a call. SKYPC sets up routers, traces weak coverage, and tidies small office networks at the site.

Say which rooms fail, and whether a computer on a cable is fast while Wi-Fi is not. That tells the technician if they are fixing coverage or the connection itself. You can book networking and Wi-Fi the same way as a laptop repair, and you pay after the work.
