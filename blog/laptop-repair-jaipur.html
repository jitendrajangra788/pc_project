Laptop screen or battery repair in Jaipur

12 Nov 2026 · SKYPC technicians

Most laptop calls in Jaipur are not a mystery fault. The picture is damaged, the machine dies off the charger, or both. Knowing which one it is saves a wasted visit and tells the technician what to bring.

Signs the screen needs a repair

A screen problem stays on the display even when the laptop is plugged in and the battery is full. Look for these before you book:

  • A crack, ink blot, or coloured lines that do not move when you restart.
  • A black panel while the keyboard lights up and you can hear the fan.
  • A very dim image that looks normal only under a torch.
  • Flicker that changes when you move the lid, which often points to the cable in the hinge.

If an external monitor shows a normal picture, the laptop itself is running and the panel or the hinge cable is the likely part. Mention that when you book a doorstep visit.

Signs the battery is failing

A battery fault is about power, not the picture. The laptop may still look perfect on the desk.

  • It shuts down as soon as the charger is pulled, even after a full night of charging.
  • The trackpad or the base feels swollen, or the lid no longer sits flat.
  • Windows reports a battery that will not charge past a low percentage.
  • The charger gets hot while the battery percentage barely moves.

A swollen battery should come out. Do not keep using the laptop pressed closed in a bag. Tell the technician it may be swollen so they bring a matching pack, not only a screen.

What happens on a doorstep visit

SKYPC comes to the home or office. The technician confirms the fault, names the part, and explains the cost before any work starts. Screen, battery, keyboard and charging faults are the usual laptop jobs. You pay after the service, and the cover on the part is confirmed at that point, not guessed on the phone.

Have the laptop model ready. It is printed on a sticker under the machine. A photo of that sticker on WhatsApp is enough for most visits.

Repair the laptop or replace it

Repair is the practical choice when the rest of the machine is still fast enough for your work and the part is available. Replacement is worth a conversation when the laptop is very old, needs a screen and a battery and a keyboard together, or cannot run the software you use even when it is healthy.

If several machines in a shop or office fail the same way every few months, a one-off repair will not stop the pattern. An annual maintenance plan is the better fit for that.
