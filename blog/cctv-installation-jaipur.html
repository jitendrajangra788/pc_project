CCTV installation for a Jaipur home or shop

4 Nov 2026 · SKYPC technicians

A camera only helps if it sees the door, the counter, or the lane you actually worry about. Extra cameras aimed at a blank wall do not make a shop safer. This is the short plan SKYPC uses before a CCTV visit in Jaipur.

Places that are worth a camera

Start with the spots where someone enters, pays, or leaves with stock. For most homes and small shops that is a short list:

  • The main gate or the shop shutter, from inside, so faces are lit.
  • The cash counter, framed so the counter is clear and the lens is not pointing into the sun.
  • A back door, terrace stair, or godown entrance if people use it when the front is closed.
  • The parking or the lane only if you can mount the camera high enough and the night light is decent.

Indoor rooms rarely need a camera unless you have asked for one for a specific reason. Tell the technician which doors matter. They will walk the site before drilling.

Decide this before the technician arrives

Three choices change the job more than the brand name on the box.

  • How many cameras, based on doors and the counter, not on a package photo.
  • Where the recorder can sit, near power, away from direct rain, and somewhere you can reach it.
  • Whether you need to watch the cameras on your phone when you are away from the shop.

Phone viewing needs a working internet connection at the recorder. If the shop Wi-Fi drops every evening, fix that first or plan a stable connection as part of the visit. The Wi-Fi guide covers the usual home and shop checks.

How phone viewing is set up

After the cameras and the recorder are in place, the technician connects the recorder to your network and installs the viewing app on your phone. You should be able to open a camera, see a recent recording, and know which password you just set. Write that password down. A system nobody can log in to is not a finished install.

Ask to see one playback from the counter camera before the technician leaves. If the face at the counter is a blur, the camera position should change while they are still there.

After the cameras are up

Check the app once a week. A camera with a spider web, a loose cable, or a full hard disk fails quietly. For a shop that depends on the footage, add the recorder to a regular visit instead of waiting for a blank screen on the day you need a clip. That is the same idea as a computer AMC: planned checks, not only emergency calls.
