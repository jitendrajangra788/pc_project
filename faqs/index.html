How do I book a service?

Book online on the Book a service page, or call / WhatsApp +91 90246 83436. We confirm the visit before arriving.

Do you come to my home or office in Jaipur?

Yes. SKYPC offers doorstep visits across Durgapura and nearby Jaipur areas. We confirm the time on a call or WhatsApp.

Do you provide laptop screen or battery replacements?

If your screen is cracked or your battery does not hold a charge, we offer component replacement using quality parts.

When do I pay for the service?

You pay after the work is finished. Parts or major repairs are explained before we proceed.

What is IT AMC service in Jaipur?

IT AMC is regular maintenance, repair, and support for computers, laptops, printers, and IT setup in Jaipur.

Why is my computer running slower than usual?

Over time, systems accumulate background processes, outdated software, and unnecessary files. We can help optimize your system's performance and ensure it runs efficiently.

Can you help with virus or malware issues?

Yes. We offer full system scans and threat removal so the computer is clean, secure, and running smoothly.

What should I do if my PC does not turn on?

First, check the power source and cables. If the issue persists, book a diagnostic so we can identify the cause.

Can you help retrieve data from a non-working device?

Yes. We recover data from hard drives, SSDs, and other storage, even if the system is not powering on.

Are you an Apple-authorised service centre?

No. SKYPC is not an Apple-authorised centre. We still help with MacBook and iMac issues where local service is possible.
